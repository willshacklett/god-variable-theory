## Limitations and Release Decision

No physical GV confirmation, independently derived constant, new force/field,
energy source or theological causal conclusion is supported. Historical speculative
texts remain with status notices. Some raw datasets and superseded classifiers
are unavailable. Finite solver checks are not convergence studies; conditional
lead medians and in-sample FPR require statistical caution. Synthetic controls
cannot exclude all real-world causes. Historical references need bibliographic
review. Other Python/platform versions are not certified.

Before approving a tag, review missing experiment-ID provenance, the new F2b
report, claim wording and verification. The unresolved records do not require
buying hardware, but should not be represented as known completed experiments.
