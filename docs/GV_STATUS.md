# GV Status

## Established

- The repository defines an operational framework using measured observables
  $X$, known-model predictions $K$, residuals $R=X-K$, and candidate statistics
  $G=f(R,\ldots)$.
- Switch and Local Tuning protocols, independently defined endpoints, ordinary
  baselines, and falsification requirements are documented.
- The experimental record includes negative and invalid results, which remain
  part of the evidence rather than being overwritten.

## Not Established

- GV existence or a distinct physical mechanism.
- A unique mathematical role or empirical effect for historical $\alpha$.
- A nontrivial universal/cross-system invariant.
- Unique activation dynamics implied by the Switch.
- Entropy repayment as an operational mechanism or established GV structure.
- A causal GV $\rightarrow A$ relationship. Local Tuning defines a measurable
  endpoint, not that causal mechanism.

Generic finite-dimensional hidden-state GV is reducible to ordinary state-space
theory. The historical equations and interpretations remain hypotheses with
unresolved dimensional, dynamical, and empirical ambiguities. Historical entropy
repayment may be investigated as a separate falsifiable hypothesis candidate.

## Next Scientific Requirement

Specify and preregister at least one held-out quantitative prediction that
outperforms the strongest ordinary model. Do not define the prediction from the
observed outcome or treat an unexplained residual as automatic evidence for GV.
No such prediction is supplied by this status document.
