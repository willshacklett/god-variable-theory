# Claims Ladder

**THE GOD VARIABLE HAS NOT BEEN CONFIRMED.**

> A residual is only a residual until known explanations are excluded.

**UNEXPLAINED ≠ GV; GV ≠ NEW PHYSICS; NEW PHYSICS ≠ GOD.**

| Level | Permitted claim | Required evidence / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LEVEL 0 - Observation | A measurable change occurred. | Calibrated measurements, uncertainty and retained data. Simulations are observations of software, not physical signals. |
| LEVEL 1 - Known-cause attribution | Timing, EM, thermal, mechanical, acoustic, software, or another known mechanism explains the change. | Intervention/model comparison consistent with a known pathway. This rejects an independent-channel claim for the event. |
| LEVEL 2 - Residual anomaly | Known tested explanations do not currently account for it. | Disclosed tested alternatives, uncertainty, multiplicity and model limits. This is incomplete attribution, not GV. |
| LEVEL 3 - Reproducible unexplained transition signature | Repeats under preregistered conditions and survives controls. | Independent repeats with locked analysis and adequate controls. Unknown ordinary explanations remain possible. |
| LEVEL 4 - GV candidate | Meets predefined GV criteria and beats relevant baselines. | All formulation-specific criteria in the [theory](THEORY.md), out-of-sample evidence and fixed success rules. Candidate is not confirmation. |
| LEVEL 5 - Novel mechanism | Independent replication and evidence justify investigating a new mechanism. | Independent apparatus/laboratory replication plus discriminating causal predictions, not merely repeated residuals. A novel effective mechanism need not be fundamental physics. |
| LEVEL 6 - Fundamental interpretation | An extraordinary interpretation merits consideration. | Extraordinary independent evidence and predictions distinguishing it from conventional and novel nonfundamental alternatives. No theological inference follows from the lower levels. |

These levels are not an automatic promotion pipeline. Record the scope of every
claim, what could refute it, and which alternatives remain. Replication of a bias
does not establish a mechanism. Negative controls or simpler-baseline wins require
demotion or rejection of the specific claim, not rhetorical escalation.

## Current Ceiling

No tracked physical dataset establishes a GV candidate. The archived
`UNEXPLAINED PROPAGATION CANDIDATE` is an intentionally injected **synthetic**
scenario. Successful clock/EM/environment gates validate encoded software logic
only. A real surviving switch signal could at most be called an unexplained
propagation candidate until additional evidence earns stronger wording.

Benchmark support, if ever earned, would support a predictive statistic in that
benchmark, not establish a new physical entity. Current fluid results are
[negative or invalid](NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md).
