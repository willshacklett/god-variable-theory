# GV Fluid Instability Benchmark — F2 Results

## Outcome

**BENCHMARK INVALID**

F2 failed its preregistered validity requirements.

## Validity Summary

- Solver finite/stable: PASS
- Control physical-event rate: 0.000 — PASS
- Active physical-event rate: 0.333 — FAIL
- Required active physical-event rate: >= 0.700
- GV t=0 alarm rate: 0.000 — PASS

Because fewer than 70% of active trajectories reached the predefined physical event, detector performance is not interpreted as evidence for or against GV.

## Scientific Interpretation

F2 did not provide a valid test of the preregistered GV hypothesis.

The numerical solver remained stable and the control population did not generate false physical events, but the active parameter regime was insufficient to produce the target instability event in the preregistered minimum fraction of trajectories.

Therefore all detector lead-time comparisons from F2 are exploratory only and must not be interpreted as SUPPORTIVE or NOT SUPPORTIVE.

No F2 detector definition, threshold rule, weighting, or success criterion will be retroactively modified.

## Next Step

A separately preregistered corrective experiment, F2b, may modify only the physical-regime parameters needed to raise active event incidence while preserving:

- the same detector definitions,
- the same control-only calibration logic,
- the same 5% detector false-positive target,
- the same GV equal-weight composite,
- and the same SUPPORTIVE / NOT SUPPORTIVE decision rule.
