# Constraint Mathematics

## Proposition 001

### Present Constraint Equivalence Does Not Imply Complete State Equivalence

Let two systems have identical present constraint sets:

C1 = C2

The systems may still possess different internal histories:

H1 != H2

Therefore equality of present constraint sets alone does not imply that the
systems have identical complete states.

A more complete representation may be:

S = (C, H)

This proposition does not establish new mathematics.

History may already be represented through dynamical systems, hidden-state
models, hysteresis, stochastic processes, memory kernels, fractional
calculus, or path-dependent functionals.

The proposition establishes only that the present allowed-state set C may
be an incomplete state description.
